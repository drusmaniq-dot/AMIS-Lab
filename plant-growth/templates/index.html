Plant Sensitivity Response GUI

A specialized analytical simulator for photobiologists, agricultural engineers, and lighting-system designers — including vertical-farming LED engineers. It evaluates how a given artificial light spectrum interacts with plant photoreceptors and photobiological sensitivity curves, computing cross-correlation, quantum-efficiency, and metabolic-yield metrics to predict plant physiological performance under diverse lighting environments, without requiring immediate physical experimentation.
